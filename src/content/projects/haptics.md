Haptics is an iOS app for real-time communication through touch, drawings, and expressive feedback. It's open source now, and [Ilia](https://x.com/iamnalimov) and I wrote a deep dive on how it works for Spotted in Prod

Technical: a highly optimized Core Animation drawing engine, Metal-based visual effects, SpriteKit scenes, and a single-source-of-truth MVVM architecture
