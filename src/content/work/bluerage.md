We started as a mobile platform for creating Mini Apps end to end with prompts. As software creation became cheaper, people who could not previously express an idea through code could suddenly build and share it

We worked closely with [Z Fellows](https://zfellows.com) while exploring the idea

We then pivoted from an iOS app to a Telegram bot built around the existing Telegram Mini Apps platform

After a few iterations, I explored an open-source, mobile-first automation tool that could think and act across your tools: an agentic take on Apple Shortcuts
